<img src="./images/lattice_icon.png" width="30" height="30"> <big> <b> Summary - Dr Lucy Whalley </b> </big> 

I am a computational scientist with expertise in research software engineering and high performance computing. In my previous role as an Associate Professor in Physics at Northumbria University I led a [research programme](https://lucydot.github.io/publications/) in computational materials science, alongside co-leading the [Materials Chemistry Consortium](https://mcc.hec.ac.uk/) (EPSRC, EP/X035859/1), the [SCALE-UP network](https://scale-up.ac.uk/) (UKRI) and [Sonic Intangibles](https://sonicintangibles.github.io/) (UKRI, MR/Z506448/1). I am now looking for new employment opportunities in technology and computing.

I am a fellow of the [Software Sustainability Institute](https://software.ac.uk/about), an Associate Editor at the [Journal of Open Source Software](https://joss.theoj.org) and chair of the UK national supercomputer Archer2 [User Advisory Group](https://www.archer2.ac.uk/about/uag/). I am interested in how we can improve research practice in the computational sciences, with a particular interest in how we can work in an open and [interdisciplinary](https://lucydot.github.io/dotspace/) way. I publish [my code](https://lucydot.github.io/software/) on Github whenever possible, and I [speak about my work](https://lucydot.github.io/talks/) to a variety of audiences.

I have [taught](http://lucydot.github.io/teaching/) a range of subjects including introductory programming, computational physics and quantum mechanics, and I co-organise the annual [Rising Stars workshop](https://www.royce.ac.uk/events/rising-stars-2026/) to support under-represented groups in Materials Science. I am also a mum to <strike>1,2,3,4,5</strike> 6 year old Robin, who I am training to be a [world class Hacker](https://twitter.com/lucydotwhalley/status/1358480776841617412/photo/1).
